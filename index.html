Microsoft Teams animated Emoji Scraper

Enter a list of animated Microsoft Teams emojis to scrape and then hit the scrape button!

Note: some emojis just randomly don't work since microsoft named them weirdly, but most seem to work.



About

Hiiii welcome to my silly website!! I really like the :oreoyum: and :emo: emojis but I couldn't find anywhere to download them online so I made this!

Everything happens locally on your device, there's not even a backend attached since Microsoft for some reason allows cross-origin requests for their emoji assets which is cool.

Uses a fork I made of gif.js to compile the emoji spritesheets into .gif. My fork simply adds support for transparency thresholds to fix broken looking alpha.
